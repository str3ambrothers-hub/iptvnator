The Android TV and Fire TV app has the StreamBros logo as its launcher icon, home-screen banner, loading screen and web app icons.

The app is named StreamBros on the Android TV and Fire TV home screen, in its loading and settings screens and in the web app's English text; the About screen still credits IPTVnator as the open-source project it is based on.
